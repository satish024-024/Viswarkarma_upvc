## 🌟 Brand positioning & Core Value Propositions

### 1. Aditya Birla & Hindalco Legacy
- **Legacy:** 150+ years of trust.
- **Authority:** Global leader in aluminium; Hindalco powers giants like Audi and Boeing.

### 2. Revolutionary Duranium™ Alloy
- **Material:** Patented Duranium™ system aluminium alloy.
- **Strength:** 40% stronger than standard aluminium.
- **Design Impact:** Enables ultra-slim, minimalist frames and larger window panels without compromising structural integrity.
- **Durability:** Cyclone-tested for long-term wind load resistance.

- **Concept:** Every window is tested and rated for its ability to resist wind load, water penetration, and air infiltration.
- **Transparency:** Features a "Know Your WiWA© Score" to build consumer confidence and help choose the perfect window for local weather conditions.

### 4. Duraslim & Duraslim Edge
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist, sleek slim frame profiles with high strength.

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Insights

### 1. Types of Windows Offered
- **Sliding** aluminium windows and doors.
- **Openable / Casement** aluminium windows and doors.
- **Ranges:** *Duraslim Edge*, *Duraslim*, and *Essentials* (catering to different budget segments).

### 2. Comparison: Aluminium vs. Wood vs. uPVC
- **Weather Resistance:** Wood and standard uPVC degrade/warp under extreme Indian heat and monsoon moisture. Eternia's Duranium™ alloy is stable and WiWA™ certified.
- **Maintenance:** Wood suffers from termites, mold, and warping; uPVC can discolor or warp in high UV index. Aluminium requires virtually zero maintenance.
- **Fire Safety:** Aluminium has a much higher melting point and is naturally non-combustible compared to uPVC and wood.
- **Aesthetics:** Sleeker profiles, aerodynamic interlocks, and availability of hundreds of powder-coated colours and wood-grain foil finishes.
