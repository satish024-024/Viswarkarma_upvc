## ⚙️ 4. "When Innovation Meets Perfection" (Image Overlays)
This section overlays 11 distinct performance indicators on a large product schematic ([single_home_big.webp](https://www.eterniawindows.com/wp-content/themes/eternia/images/home-new/single_home_big.webp)):

* **Enhanced Security:** Multi-point locking mechanisms.
* **Rugged Reliability:** 40% stronger than standard aluminium.
* **Storm Proof Strength:** Designed to withstand cyclonic winds.
* **Wider Views:** Larger panel sizes.
* **No Rattling:** Sturdy quality accessories & woolpile.
* **Noise Insulation:** High quality gaskets & fin based woolpile.
* **No Drips or Leaks:** Efficient water drainage system.
* **Peace of Mind:** 12-year brand warranty on profiles + 5 years warranty on hardware.
* **High Quality Sealing:** Silicated fin-based wool pile to block out water, dust & sound.
* **Smooth Operation:** 1L+ Cycle tested rollers & hinges.
* **Made for Indian Conditions:** Collapsible integrated pest mesh.
