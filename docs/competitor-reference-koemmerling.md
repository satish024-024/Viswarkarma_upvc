# Competitor Reference: Koemmerling India (uPVC Windows with Mosquito Mesh)

This document contains competitor knowledge and technical references compiled from **Koemmerling India** (part of the profine Group), specializing in premium uPVC window and door systems.

---

## 🌟 Brand Positioning & Core Value Propositions
* **Group:** profine Group (global manufacturer of premium uPVC profiles).
* **Corporate Value:** Focus on sustainability, circular economy, and eco-friendly (lead-free) compounding.
* **Key Use Case:** Secure ventilation (appreciating natural airflow without compromising on bug/pest entry or intruder security).

---

## 🔩 Technical Specs & Features

### 1. 6-Chambered Profile Design
* **Benefit:** Multi-chamber extrusion (6-chambered design) provides superior acoustic damping, structural strength, and thermal resistance (minimizing heat transmission into the home).

### 2. Integrated Grill & Mesh Systems
* **Design:** Built-in pre-fitted mosquito net channels that slide or lock seamlessly inside the uPVC frame. Unlike wooden frames with custom-stapled wire meshes, these are part of a multi-track extruded sash system.
* **Intruder Defense:** Ability to support an integrated metal security grill alongside the mosquito mesh. This lets homeowners leave windows open at night (for cross-ventilation in ground-floor bedrooms or nurseries) without risking security compromises.

### 3. Multi-Point Locking
* **Benefit:** Multiple locking pins engage simultaneously along the frame sash on single handle actuation. Increases security and pulls the window tightly against EPDM gaskets for acoustic and thermal sealing when closed.

### 4. Eco-Friendly Compounding
* **Benefit:** Profiles engineered with high-impact resistance and stabilized using green, eco-friendly calcium-zinc compound instead of lead. Prevents yellowing/discoloration in intense UV index regions.

---

## 🏠 Key Use-Case Scenarios
* **Nurseries / Children’s Bedrooms:** Safe airflow without mosquitoes or pests during sleep hours.
* **Kitchens:** High cross-ventilation to evacuate heat/smoke while keeping out insects drawn to food.
* **Wet Rooms / Bathrooms:** Natural airflow to reduce humidity and mold build-up while maintaining bug security.
