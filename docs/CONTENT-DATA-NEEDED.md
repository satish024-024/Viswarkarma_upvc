# Business Content & Data Needed Checklist

This is a structured checklist of all real business information, assets, and pricing rules needed from the business owner to finalize the uPVC & Aluminium platform.

---

## 📞 1. Contact & Identity Information
- [ ] **Official Business Name** (e.g., "Daddy uPVC & Aluminium", or official registered trade name)
- [ ] **Call Phone Number** (Primary customer service number)
- [ ] **WhatsApp Number** (Cell number that will receive the compiled configurator messages)
- [ ] **Email Address** (e.g., info@businessname.com)
- [ ] **Physical Address** (Factory location in Peenya and any display showrooms)
- [ ] **Business Hours** (e.g., Mon-Sat: 9 AM - 7 PM, Sun: Closed)
- [ ] **Google Maps Embed Link / Co-ordinates** (For factory map locator on the Contact page)

---

## 🎨 2. Media & Branding Assets
- [ ] **Company Logo** (High-res SVG, PNG, or Vector format)
- [ ] **Hero Media Asset** (A premium video loop showing extrusion machines or installers sealing window apertures, or high-res photographs of villas)
- [ ] **Factory Photographs** (Peenya production line photos showing corner fusion welding, profile extrusion bays, or glass assembly areas)
- [ ] **Project Gallery Photos** (Photos of actual completed sites in Bangalore/Karnataka)
  - Villa uPVC wood-finish installations
  - Penthouse sliding glass panels
  - Balcony frameless railing setups
  - Commercial storefronts / elevation glass
- [ ] **Profile Brand Specifications** (Are you using AIS Windows, Fenesta profiles, Aluplast, Deceuninck, VEKA, NCL, or custom extrusions?)

---

## 📏 3. uPVC & Aluminium Product Specs
- [ ] **uPVC Profile Series List** (Confirm the thickness naming, e.g., 60mm, 88mm, 112mm, or custom brand series)
- [ ] **Aluminium Profile Naming** (e.g., 50mm Slimline, 90mm Thermal Break, 120mm Lift-and-Slide)
- [ ] **Color Options Offered** (Confirm availability of foil types: White, Anthracite Grey, Golden Oak, Dark Walnut, etc.)
- [ ] **Glass Options List** (Single glazed 5mm, Single 6mm Toughened, Double Glazed DGU, Laminated Safety Glass, Frosted, Tinted, Solar reflective)
- [ ] **Mosquito Mesh Options** (Fiberglass invisible mesh, pleated sliding accordion mesh, SS304 structural security mesh)
- [ ] **Locking Hardware Brands & Types** (Siegenia, Kinlong, Giesse, Crescent locks, Multi-point espagnolette locks, Key-locking handles)

---

## 🧮 4. Pricing Rules & Estimation Modifiers
- [ ] **Baseline Price per Sq. Ft.** for each profile and window type:
  - uPVC Sliding Window (₹/sq.ft)
  - uPVC Casement Window (₹/sq.ft)
  - uPVC Fixed Window (₹/sq.ft)
  - Aluminium Sliding Window (₹/sq.ft)
  - Aluminium Casement Window (₹/sq.ft)
- [ ] **Series Price Modifiers** (Added fee per sq.ft. for premium/heavy sashes)
- [ ] **Colour Foil Multiplier** (Foil price surcharge percentage, e.g., +20% for Grey, +25% for Wood finish)
- [ ] **Glass Surcharge per Sq. Ft.** (Double Glazed Units vs. Single sheet, toughening fees)
- [ ] **Mesh Surcharge per Sq. Ft.** (SS304 vs. pleated mesh cost)
- [ ] **Locking Hardware Flat Cost** (Flat price added per window/door unit for multi-point lock upgrades)
- [ ] **Installation Fee Guidelines** (e.g., flat ₹1,500 per unit, or ₹60 per sq. ft., or based on height/floor level)

---

## 📝 5. Editorial Copy & Social Proof
- [ ] **About Us Heritage Copy** (More details on how the family-run business started 25+ years ago, credentials, and factory setup)
- [ ] **Testimonials** (Quotes, names, project configurations, and Bangalore locations of previous happy clients)
- [ ] **Service Areas list** (Served localities inside Bangalore, Mysuru, Tumakuru, and Mangaluru)
- [ ] **Common FAQ Questions** (Specific warnings or tips for local masonry openings, monsoon sealing, or glass strength standards)
